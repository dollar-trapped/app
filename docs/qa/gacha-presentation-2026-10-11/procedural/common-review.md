## Material quality limits that remain

- The case still reads as a clean vector illustration rather than a richly shaded physical prop. Large, mostly uniform lid/front panels and simple fittings limit perceived material depth.
- Impact smoke is still a diffuse pale haze in the sampled arrival frame. It signals arrival but does not demonstrate detailed rolling dust or smoke. The cached rendering approach cannot be validated from images as a device-performance improvement.
- The falling object is a flat dollar glyph with a particle trail. It gives the app an identifiable theme, but should not be described as a fully three-dimensional falling star.

These are visual style/finish limits, not demonstrated functional blockers. This review supports the concrete improvements above, not a claim of parity with major commercial gacha cinematics.
